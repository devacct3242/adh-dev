Questions

When does suhoor end?

At Fajr. The “Fajr” column is the time to stop eating and drinking.

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

The first fast is on Monday, February 8, 2027, Eid al-Fitr on Tuesday, March 9, 2027. The dates are calculated and may shift by a day with the official announcement.

When is Tarawih prayed?

After the Isha prayer. The “Isha” column is the earliest time Tarawih can start.

When is the Eid prayer?

About 30–45 minutes after sunrise (06:50); mosques announce the exact time.
